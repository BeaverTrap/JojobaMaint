​Fire Prevention & Canopy Raising: Outer Perimeter Behind Storage

Exterior grounds behind the storage facility

​This week, we started tackling the pepper trees out beyond the main gate, just behind the storage area. While this gives the exterior grounds a much cleaner look, a major part of this project is fire prevention. We are actively clearing out the dead brush and removing all the suckers to raise the canopy to a nice, high level, creating a solid fire break along that edge of the park. ​Here is a quick breakdown of the effort so far:

​Site Prep & Heavy Lifting: John James has been running the big John Deere backhoe tractor to dig out a hole in the area to make the workflow smoother.

​Tractor Training: While working the site, John James has also been teaching Jon Wayne on the John Deere. Jon has been getting some great hands-on practice using the front clamps to pull and clear out the felled branches.

​Note: This is an ongoing project. I will be updating this post and adding more pictures to the gallery as we make further progress!
